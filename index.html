An enemy dreadnought hides in the nebula. Place your fleet on the grid, push into the fog as it lifts, and sink their flagship before the war clock runs out.

1 · DEPLOYPlace your ships in the three rows nearest you, on any of the four Z-layers.
2 · SCOUTFog lifts from random sectors each turn. A ship that enters a sector clears it.
3 · ENGAGEEvery ship moves one block and attacks each turn. Pick a weapon, tap a target. Hitting the same target twice builds a combo. DOGFIGHT flies it yourself.
4 · SURVIVEWins bring loot. Losses and retreats send ships to the repair bay for later operations.
